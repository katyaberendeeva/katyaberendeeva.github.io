01 · REVISE

Собираем знакомые времена

Present Simple
V / V-s

Привычки, факты, регулярные действия.

She studies every evening.

− She doesn’t study on Sundays.

? Does she study every day?

Present Continuous
am / is / are + V-ing

Действие происходит сейчас или ситуация временная.

She is studying now.

− She isn’t studying now.

? Is she studying now?

Past Simple
V-ed / V₂

Завершённое действие в прошлом.

She studied yesterday.

− She didn’t study yesterday.

? Did she study yesterday?

Quick revision — выбери правильную форму

1. My sister usually ___ (read) before bed, but today she ___ (watch) a documentary.

Введи две формы через точку с запятой.

2. We ___ (not / see) the exhibition last weekend because it ___ (be) closed.

3. ___ your cousin usually ___ (take) the bus, or ___ she ___ (walk) to school?

Запиши четыре формы через точку с запятой.

4. At 8 p.m. yesterday, I ___ (do) my homework. (Пока это диагностический пример перед новой темой.)

Ключи: 1) reads; is watching. 2) didn’t see; was. 3) Does; take; does; walk. 4) was doing — это уже Past Continuous, поэтому использовать как мостик к новой теме, не считать ошибкой по пройденному материалу.
02 · NEW

Past Continuous

Когда используем: действие было в процессе в определённый момент в прошлом; фон для другого события; два действия происходили параллельно.

+
was / were + V-ing

I was reading at 9 p.m.

They were talking.

−
wasn't / weren't + V-ing

I wasn’t reading.

They weren’t talking.

?
Was / Were + subject + V-ing?

Were they talking?

Was she working?

ПодлежащееФорма beПример
I / he / she / itwasShe was waiting when I arrived.
you / we / theywereThey were walking at 6.

Сравни: I was walking home when it started to rain. Длительный фон — Past Continuous; короткое событие — Past Simple.

5. While we ___ (walk) through the city, it ___ (start) to rain.

6. At 10 yesterday, he ___ (not / sleep). He ___ (prepare) for the test.

Ключи: 5) were walking; started. 6) wasn’t sleeping; was preparing.
03 · NEW

Future Simple

Когда используем: спонтанное решение, обещание или предложение; прогноз/мнение о будущем. Для планов и намерений часто нужны другие конструкции, их разберём отдельно.

+
will + V

I will call you later.

−
will not / won’t + V

I won’t forget.

?
Will + subject + V?

Will you help me?

Важно: после will используем начальную форму глагола без to и без окончания -s: She will come, не will comes.

7. I think people ___ (use) more renewable energy in the future.

8. “I can’t carry these bags.” — “I ___ (help) you.”

Ключи: 7) will use. 8) will help.
04 · NEW

Present Perfect

Когда используем: прошлое действие связано с настоящим; жизненный опыт без указания точного завершённого времени; результат важен сейчас; период ещё не закончился.

+
have / has + V₃

I have finished.

She has seen this film.

−
haven't / hasn't + V₃

I haven’t finished.

?
Have / Has + subject + V₃?

Have you ever visited London?

SubjectВспомогательный глаголПример
I / you / we / theyhaveThey have already arrived.
he / she / ithasShe has just arrived.

Частые маркеры: already, just, yet, ever, never, recently, so far. Не ставим Present Perfect с конкретным завершённым временем типа yesterday / last year / in 2022 — там обычно Past Simple.

Неправильные формы для запоминания: be–was/were–been; do–did–done; see–saw–seen; go–went–gone; write–wrote–written; take–took–taken; make–made–made; know–knew–known.

9. She ___ already ___ (write) the email, so you can send it.

10. ___ you ever ___ (see) a live performance by this band?

Ключи: 9) has; written. 10) Have; seen.

A. There are a lot of different studies that come out about coffee, yet it is still difficult for researchers to agree whether the drink is good for one’s health. There is not enough data to change the guidelines to include more cups of coffee. Drinking two to four cups of coffee a day will usually have positive effects. However, heavy caffeine intake — more than four cups of coffee a day — can cause insomnia, stomach upset, muscle tremors, and restlessness. And this is not a complete list of possible health problems.

B. Studies have shown that coffee provides other health benefits besides the caffeine boost and the rich flavour — like preventing diabetes and certain cancers. Drinking at least one cup of coffee a day could prevent heart problems. Researchers analyzed the coffee-drinking habits and health histories of thousands of women for 10 years. They found out that women who drank one or more cups of coffee a day were nearly 25% less likely to have a stroke than those who drank less than one cup of coffee a day.

C. The Arabica coffee plant appeared around 600,000 to 1 million years ago, when two other coffee species crossbred in the forests of what is now Ethiopia. It does not matter when it developed, but this hybrid genome enabled the plant to grow successfully as it was farmed across the world. It was believed to have been grown by humans in Ethiopia and then traded to the Middle East, where it became a well-known drink by the 15th century. Arabica coffee now provides about 60% of the world’s coffee supply.

D. There are lots of legends connected with the Arabica coffee plant spreading. According to one legend, an Indian pilgrim took secretly seven seeds out of Yemen and established coffee farms in India around 1670. Dutch traders began farming the plant in other regions. They first planted the Arabica coffee plant on the island of Java, and one was sent to a botanical garden in Amsterdam in 1706. The Dutch shared a plant with the French, who also transported seedlings to their colonies in the 18th century.

E. Coffee is one of the world’s most widely-consumed beverages, yet individuals may love it or hate it. For some, coffee is the true nectar of life, while others will not touch a drop of the stuff. Now, a new study reveals how taste preferences influence people’s preferences for a cup of coffee. The findings boost our understanding of what motivates regular coffee consumption. It could explain why the same hit of caffeine is what motivates regular coffee consumption. (The printed scan contains a damaged line in this paragraph.)

F. When it comes to great flavour, coffee chemistry plays a critical role in roasting and brewing. During roasting, oil locked inside the beans rises to the surface. It can appear at around 400 degrees. The more oil, the stronger the flavour of the coffee. Caffeine content gets affected by the water spent more time in contact with the coffee grounds. So regular coffee often has more caffeine than espresso or cappuccino. Thus, great coffee depends on roasting and brewing rather than additives or flavourings.

G. For those who like the taste of coffee but can’t stand the caffeine, scientists have created a variety of ways to extract caffeine from coffee beans. In the most common method, called “solvent extraction”, the beans are steamed to raise their moisture content. Dissolved caffeine rises to the surface of the beans and is washed off using an organic solvent. The beans are then dried off and ready to go — to make coffee. In fact, decaffeinated coffee still contains caffeine, but in a very small amount.

Почему выбрали эти заголовки? Ищем доказательство в тексте

A → 5. Friend or foe. Подсказка: “whether the drink is good for one’s health”; далее перечислены возможные отрицательные эффекты чрезмерного потребления кофе. Автор показывает и пользу, и риски — кофе может быть другом или врагом.

B → 7. A healthy drink. Подсказки: “provides other health benefits”, “preventing diabetes and certain cancers”, “less likely to have a stroke”. Абзац сосредоточен на возможной пользе для здоровья.

C → 3. How it all started. Подсказки: “The Arabica coffee plant appeared…” и “grown by humans in Ethiopia and then traded to the Middle East”. Текст рассказывает о происхождении растения и начале распространения кофе.

D → 2. Stories behind. Подсказки: “There are lots of legends…” и “According to one legend…”. В абзаце рассказываются легенды и исторические истории о распространении кофе.

E → 1. Tastes differ. Подсказки: “love it or hate it” и “taste preferences”. Главная мысль — люди по-разному относятся к вкусу кофе. Часть печатной строки повреждена, поэтому опираемся только на читаемые слова.

F → 8. It’s all in the cooking. Подсказки: “roasting and brewing”, “During roasting…” и “great coffee depends on roasting and brewing”. Абзац объясняет, как обжарка и приготовление влияют на вкус.

G → 4. Is it real coffee? Подсказки: “extract caffeine from coffee beans” и “decaffeinated coffee still contains caffeine”. Текст объясняет способ удаления кофеина и уточняет, что в кофе без кофеина он всё же остаётся в малом количестве.

В этом режиме соответствующие фразы подсвечены непосредственно в тексте. Сначала найди доказательство, затем сравни его с объяснением.

06 · HOMEWORK

Домашняя работа · 10 заданий в день

Все задания ниже тренируют только времена, изученные к концу этого урока: Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, Past Continuous, Future Simple, Present Perfect.

Day 1 · Choose the tense / form

  1. Researchers ___ (study) coffee-drinking habits for ten years. (Past Simple — действие завершено в прошлом в контексте текста.)
  2. At the moment, scientists ___ (look) at how taste preferences influence coffee consumption.
  3. Arabica coffee ___ (provide) about 60% of the world’s coffee supply. (Present Simple)
  4. Yesterday, I ___ (read) an article about caffeine.
  5. At 8 p.m. yesterday, I ___ (read) the article.
  6. I think scientists ___ (find) more information in the future.
  7. She ___ already ___ (finish) the reading task.
  8. They usually ___ (drink) coffee in the morning, but today they ___ (choose) tea.
  9. While the researchers ___ (analyse) the data, they ___ (notice) a pattern.
  10. ___ you ever ___ (try) decaffeinated coffee?
Возможные ответы: 1 studied / have studied — по контексту «for ten years» без завершённой точки возможны оба, поэтому для однозначности в проверке обсудить контекст; 2 are looking; 3 provides; 4 read; 5 was reading; 6 will find; 7 has already finished; 8 drink / are choosing; 9 were analysing / noticed; 10 Have / tried.

Day 2 · Build the form

  1. Make negative: Coffee provides health benefits. → __________________
  2. Make a question: The researchers analysed the data. → __________________
  3. Make negative: She is reading the article now. → __________________
  4. Make a question: They were discussing the results at noon. → __________________
  5. Make negative: I will forget the new words. → __________________
  6. Make a question: He has visited this museum. → __________________
  7. Complete: While I ___ (walk), I ___ (see) a coffee shop.
  8. Complete: She ___ never ___ (try) this drink.
  9. Complete: We ___ (not / meet) them last week.
  10. Complete: I’m sure you ___ (do) well in the exam.
Ответы: 1 Coffee doesn’t provide… 2 Did the researchers analyse…? 3 She isn’t reading… 4 Were they discussing…? 5 I won’t forget… 6 Has he visited…? 7 was walking / saw 8 has / tried 9 didn’t meet 10 will do.

Day 3 · Mixed tenses in context

  1. People often ___ (have) different opinions about coffee.
  2. Look! The researcher ___ (explain) the results.
  3. When I arrived, the lesson ___ already ___ (start). (Use Past Simple/Past Continuous only if Past Perfect has not been taught: write “was already in progress” or ask teacher.)
  4. Last year, my family ___ (travel) to a place famous for coffee.
  5. At this time yesterday, we ___ (visit) a museum.
  6. I ___ never ___ (read) this text before.
  7. Don’t worry, I ___ (help) you with the task.
  8. She ___ (not / like) coffee, so she usually drinks tea.
  9. What ___ you ___ (do) when the teacher came in?
  10. They ___ just ___ (finish) their project.
Ответы: 1 have 2 is explaining 3 was already in progress (Past Perfect intentionally excluded) 4 travelled/traveled 5 were visiting 6 have / read 7 will help 8 doesn’t like 9 were / doing 10 have / finished.

Day 4 · Exam-style control

  1. Choose the correct form: When we arrived at the café, it ___ (be) closed. Запиши форму глагола, затем объясни выбор по алгоритму: 1) найди указание на время/ситуацию; 2) назови выбранное время; 3) объясни, какое значение оно передаёт в этом предложении.
  2. At present, many researchers ___ (investigate) the effects of coffee.
  3. My brother ___ (not / drink) coffee every day.
  4. We ___ (see) this documentary two days ago.
