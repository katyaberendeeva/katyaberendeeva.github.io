60-minute lesson

My New Life

Ученица: Лиза, 20 лет · Present Simple vs Past Simple
060 min

    1. Warm-up

    Мягкое включение в английский, активация обоих времён
    5 min

    Ask Liza

    • How are you today?
    • How was your week?
    • What do you usually do during the week?
    • What did you do yesterday?
    • What's new in your life?
    Teacher note: не поправляй здесь ошибки — просто слушай и мысленно отмечай, в каком времени Лиза чувствует себя увереннее. Это твоя отправная точка для урока.

    2. Speaking — My New Job

    Массаж и завод: сравниваем «сейчас» и «раньше»
    10 min
    Present Simple

    NOW

    • Where do you work?
    • What do you usually do at work?
    • What does a normal working day look like?
    • What do you like about your job?
    • What's difficult?
    Past Simple

    BEFORE

    • Where did you work before?
    • Why did you choose this job?
    • What did you expect before you started?
    • What surprised you?
    Useful language I work as a massage therapist / I also work at a factory / my shift starts at… / before that, I worked at… / I didn't expect…
    Teacher note: не исправляй каждую ошибку в моменте — она собьёт Лизу с мысли. Запиши 4–6 характерных ошибок (например, путаница do/does, пропуск -ed) и разбери их в блоке Grammar или в конце урока.

    exhausted

    3. Tell me when you last felt exhausted.
    shift
    1. Make a sentence with "shift".
    2. Ask a question with "shift".
    3. Tell me about your shifts.
    flexible

    2. Ask a question with "flexible".
    3. Tell me what is flexible in your schedule.
    routine

    to relax

    4. Grammar — Present Simple vs Past Simple

    Быстрое напоминание + практика на её собственных примерах
    10 min
    Present SimplePast Simple
    usuallyyesterday
    every daylast week
    alwaystwo days ago
    normallyin 2025
    oftenwhen I was…

    A. Choose the correct form

    1. Liza (work / works) as a massage therapist.
    2. Yesterday she (finish / finished) her shift late.
    3. She usually (walk / walks) her dog in the evening.
    4. Last week she (start / started) a new schedule at the factory.
    5. She (doesn't / didn't) like her old job.
    Answer key
    1. works  ·  2. finished  ·  3. walks  ·  4. started  ·  5. didn't

    B. Explain why

    Ask Liza to explain, in her own words, why sentences 2 and 4 above are Past Simple, and why 1 and 3 are Present Simple.

    C. Make it personal

    Liza writes/says 2–3 sentences about her own life, one in Present Simple and one in Past Simple:

    Prompt Every day I… / Yesterday I…
    Teacher note: используй ошибки, замеченные в блоке Speaking, как материал для пункта B — так грамматика привязывается к её собственной речи, а не к абстрактным примерам.

    5. Irregular Verbs — Quick Drill

    go → went → I went to work yesterday.
    5 min
    Teacher note: называй глагол в инфинитиве, Лиза говорит форму прошедшего времени и сразу составляет с ней своё предложение. Не задерживайся на одном глаголе больше 30 секунд.
    go → went→your own sentence
    come → came→your own sentence
    get → got→your own sentence
    have → had→your own sentence
    do → did→your own sentence
    make → made→your own sentence
    see → saw→your own sentence
    take → took→your own sentence
    give → gave→your own sentence
    feel → felt→your own sentence

    6. Listening

    A day in the life of someone working two jobs
    10 min
    Teacher note: подбери конкретное видео заранее (1–3 минуты, уровень A2/B1, естественная речь, не детский контент). Если не нашлось готового ролика, используй эти запросы на YouTube:
    "day in my life" massage therapist working two jobs daily routine vlog my daily routine vlog English

    Before listening — 1 min

    1. What do you think this person's job is like?
    2. Do you think their day is similar to yours?

    While listening — 5 min

    1. Multiple choice: pick the correct option based on what you hear (prepare 1–2 based on the video).
    2. True / False: 2–3 statements about the video.
    3. Find specific information: what time does their day start / what do they do first?
    4. Present or Past? Liza notes down 2 things the speaker does every day (Present) and 2 things that happened on that particular day (Past).

    After listening — 4 min

    1. What was the most surprising part of their day?
    2. Is your daily routine similar or different? How?
    3. Would you like to try their job for one day? Why / why not?

    7. Final Speaking — A Day in My Life

    Свободный рассказ с использованием обоих времён
    8 min

    Task: Tell me about a normal day in your life. Use both Present Simple and Past Simple — for example, describe yesterday specifically at some point.

    Support words usually · every day · in the morning · at work · after work · yesterday · last week · then · after that · in the evening

    Follow-up questions

    1. What did you do yesterday?
    2. What do you usually do after work?
    3. What did you eat yesterday?
    4. What do you usually do with your dog?
    Teacher note: после рассказа дай короткий устный feedback по 2–3 самым важным ошибкам — не больше. Это последний speaking-блок, важно закончить на позитивной ноте.

    8. Wrap-up

    Рефлексия, без нового материала
    5 min
    1. What was easy today?
    2. What was difficult?
    3. Tell me one sentence about your life in Present Simple.
    4. Tell me one sentence about yesterday in Past Simple.

    • Какие irregular verbs вызывают трудности
    • Насколько свободно она говорит

    Common Mistakes

    Типичные ошибки русскоязычных учеников
    reference
    ✗ IncorrectShe work at a factory.
    ✓ CorrectShe works at a factory.
    ✗ IncorrectShe didn't liked the schedule.
    ✓ CorrectShe didn't like the schedule.
    ✗ IncorrectWhere you worked before?
    ✓ CorrectWhere did you work before?
    ✗ IncorrectYesterday I go to work early.
    ✓ CorrectYesterday I went to work early.
    ✗ IncorrectI have 20 years.
    ✓ CorrectI am 20 years old.
    ✗ IncorrectI have a dog since two years.
    ✓ CorrectI've had a dog for two years. (mention, don't drill — future topic)
    Teacher note: не разбирай все ошибки за один урок — выбери 4–6 самых частых у Лизы конкретно и вернись к остальным на будущих уроках.

    Teacher's Note

    Что проверить и куда двигаться дальше
    after class
    • Проверить, свободно ли Лиза строит вопросы в Past Simple (did + base form) — обычно самое слабое место.
    • Отметить, использует ли она -s в третьем лице в Present Simple без напоминания.
    • Собрать список irregular verbs, которые вызвали заминку, для отдельного повторения в начале следующего урока.
    • Если Present Simple vs Past Simple уже устойчивы — следующая тема может стать Present Continuous или Present Perfect (для рассказа об опыте на новой работе).
    • Учитывая её реальную занятость (массаж + завод), в будущих уроках можно ввести лексику про тайм-менеджмент и усталость/восстановление — это близко её жизни и мотивирует говорить.
