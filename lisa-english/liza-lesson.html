    🎧 Listening

    A real video about someone's daily routine
    10 min
    My Daily Routine — Easy English Listening (A2)
    Short video · slow, clear English · subtitles available
    ▶️ Watch video

    Before listening

    1. What do you think a "typical day" video usually shows?
    2. Guess: what time does she wake up?

    While listening

    1. Write down 2 things she does every day.
    2. Write down 2 activities in the order she mentions them.
    3. True or false: she talks about her morning and her evening.

    After listening

    1. Is your daily routine similar or different?
    2. What's one thing you would change about her day?
    3. What did YOU do at the same time yesterday?
