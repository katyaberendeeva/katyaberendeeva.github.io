03 · reading

Superstitions around the world

10 min

Read the short text. Remember: these are examples of beliefs, not facts about what really causes luck.

Example 1

A black cat

In some places, people say that a black cat crossing your path brings bad luck. In other places, black cats are seen as lucky. So the same animal can mean different things to different people.

Example 2

A broken mirror

One old superstition says that breaking a mirror brings seven years of bad luck. Some people avoid looking into a broken mirror because of this belief. Other people think it is just a story.

Example 3

A four-leaf clover

Most clovers have three leaves, so a four-leaf clover is unusual. Many people see it as a symbol of good luck. Some keep a picture or a small charm of a clover before an important day.

05 · grammar

Should, shouldn’t and might

8 min

Use these words to talk about advice and possibility.

should / shouldn’t
Advice: “You should take your lucky pen.”
Negative advice: “You shouldn’t worry about a black cat.”
might
Possibility, not certainty: “It might rain tomorrow.”
“A lucky charm might make you feel calmer.”
Объяснение по-русски: чем отличаются should, shouldn’t и might?
  • should — стоит, следует: даём совет или говорим, что правильно сделать. You should rest. — Тебе стоит отдохнуть.
  • shouldn’t — не стоит, не следует: советуем не делать что-то. You shouldn’t worry. — Тебе не стоит переживать.
  • might — возможно, может быть: говорим о возможности, но не уверены. It might rain. — Возможно, будет дождь.
Форма после них: should / shouldn’t / might + глагол в начальной форме без to и без окончания -s: should go, shouldn’t worry, might happen. Не говорим should to go или she mights. Вопрос: Should I go? Отрицание: might not happen.

06 · dialogue

Before an important exam 💬

9 min

Read the dialogue aloud. Then swap roles and change the details.

NINA: Are you ready for the English test?

MAX: Almost. I’m a bit nervous.

NINA: Do you believe in lucky things?

MAX: A little. I always use the same pen for important tests.

NINA: Does it really bring you luck?

MAX: I don’t know, but it makes me feel confident.

NINA: Well, you should get some sleep and eat breakfast.

MAX: You’re right. And you should cross your fingers for me!

NINA: Of course. Good luck!

Useful expressions
