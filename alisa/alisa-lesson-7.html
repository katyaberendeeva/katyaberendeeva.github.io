03 · practice

What would you do first? 📝

8 min

Complete each sentence orally with a phrase from Step 2.

  1. When my room is a mess, it’s difficult for me to ______.
  2. I often ______ boring tasks until the last minute.
  3. When I feel overwhelmed, I usually ______.
  4. One small habit that helps me ______ is ______.
  5. I want to be more consistent with ______.
  6. At the weekend, I usually run errands, such as ______.
Possible answers

focus / put off / take a break / feel better / exercise or studying / grocery shopping, doing laundry, paying bills.

Choose the natural phrase

1. I did / made the laundry. 2. I need to run / make some errands. 3. I put off / put out cleaning my room.

Check

do the laundry · run errands · put off cleaning

04 · speaking cards

What does your reset look like? 🌸

12 min

Pick three cards. Tell a real story or explain what you would do. Add details: when, why and how it makes you feel.

🧊 The fridge situationYou open your fridge and realise it’s almost empty. What would you buy? Would you cook or order food?
🧺 The laundry mountainYour laundry basket is overflowing and you have nothing to wear. How do you deal with it?
🧾 The scary pileYou have bills, emails and small tasks you’ve been avoiding for weeks. What’s your first step?
🌷 Flowers for no reasonWould you buy yourself flowers? What small thing feels like self-love to you?
🧘 The mental resetYou’ve had a stressful week. What helps you feel calmer and more grounded?
📌 The vision boardWould you make a vision board? What three pictures or goals would you put on it?
Follow-up questions

Why does this work for you? / How often do you do it? / Have you always been like this? / What would you change? / Does it make you feel more relaxed or productive?

Teacher notes / expected points

She used to think she needed a total life reset and had to change everything. Her realistic reset includes groceries, cleaning and errands. She cooks good food when overwhelmed. Completing a small task gives satisfaction and motivation. She enjoys arranging flowers. A clean kitchen makes the morning easier. She dropped off clothes for donation and bought trash bags. She goes to therapy weekly and values mental health. A digital board can be used as laptop wallpaper and seen daily. Don't go overboard or feel you must completely change yourself.

After watching: Do you agree that small actions can create motivation? What is one realistic thing you could do this week to make life feel a little lighter?
