01 · warm-up

Let's talk about the video ☕

8 min

Before we do anything else, let's just talk. Answer naturally — you don't need to prepare perfect sentences.

1. Did you enjoy the video? Why / why not?
2. How much did you understand — 50%, 70% or 90%?
3. Was the video too slow, too fast or just right?
4. Did you understand every word? Did you have to guess anything from context?
5. Tell me 3 things you remember from the video.
6. Could you understand me if I spoke at a normal speed today?
7. Would you like to try a short normal-speed conversation?
⚡ Mini challenge: Let's talk for one minute at a natural speed. Mistakes are welcome.

⭐ Bonus grammar · keep it simple

should have + V3 = «мне надо было сделать, но я не сделал(а)».
I should have brought sunscreen.

would have + V3 = «я бы сделал(а) это в прошлом, если бы условия были другими».
I would have brought sunscreen, but I had already put it on.

would = «я бы» в воображаемой ситуации.
If I weren't afraid, I would travel more.

Say one: Last weekend I should have ______. / If I weren't afraid, I would ______.

05 · speaking time

Now let’s really talk 🗣️

18 min

Part 1 · Questions from the video

1. What is the weather like where you are right now?
2. Do you prefer the beach or the mountains? Why?
3. What's something you always bring to the beach?
4. When was the last time you went swimming?
5. If you could travel anywhere in the world right now, where would you go? And why?
↗ Keep the conversation going: After each answer, ask one extra question: Why? Who with? What happened next? How did you feel?

Part 2 · My perfect beach day 🌴

Imagine you have a completely free day tomorrow. Plan your perfect beach day.

  • Where are you? Who are you with?
  • What's in your beach bag?
  • What's the weather like? What about the water and the sand?
  • What do you do there?
  • What happens at the end of the day?

Challenge: use at least 6 new words.

"I spent the day at the beach. The weather was..."
