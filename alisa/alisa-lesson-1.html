01 · Just Talk ☕

Before we do anything, let's just talk. Teacher will ask you a few natural questions. You don't need to prepare.

What's your mood today?

Just answer and keep the conversation going.

02 · You're in this situation 👀

You're sitting in a café in Bali.

Someone sitting nearby smiles at you and starts a conversation.

“Hey, is this seat free?”

Your turn. Answer naturally. Don't prepare.

🆘 QUESTIONS — open if you need help

Where are you from?
Откуда ты? — Simple way to get to know someone.

Is this your first time in Bali?
Ты впервые на Бали? — Easy question when you are both travelling.

How long have you been here?
Как давно ты здесь?

What brings you to Bali?
Что привело тебя на Бали? — A natural way to ask why someone came.

03 · Build Your English 🧩

What did you want to say but couldn't?

Take 2–4 things you needed in the conversation. Build them into natural English.

04 · Make It Yours 🔥

Use the new language in your own sentences.

I'm still looking for…

Я всё ещё ищу…

→ I'm still looking for a good place to eat.

I've been here for…

Я здесь уже…

→ I've been here for two weeks.

05 · Same Situation — Try Again 🔄

Back to the café. This time, use at least two things from your new English.

“Hey, is this seat free?”

Your turn. Answer naturally. Try to make the conversation last longer than the first time.

🆘 QUESTIONS — open if you need help

Where are you from? — Откуда ты?

Is this your first time in Bali? — Ты впервые на Бали?

How long have you been here? — Как давно ты здесь?

What brings you to Bali? — Что привело тебя на Бали?

🟡 USEFUL PHRASES — open if you get stuck

That's actually pretty cool. — Это вообще-то довольно круто.

Really? Tell me more. — Правда? Расскажи подробнее.

How did you get into that? — Как ты этим увлёкся? / Как ты к этому пришёл?

What about you? — А ты?
