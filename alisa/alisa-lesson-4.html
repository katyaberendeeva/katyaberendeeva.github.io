✨ Small but important · polite English

Could I get…? — вежливо попросить что-то: Could I get an extra pillow, please? — Можно мне дополнительную подушку?

I'd like… — вежливо сказать, что вы хотите: I'd like to check out, please. — Я бы хотела выехать.

doesn't seem to be working звучит мягче, чем is broken: The AC doesn't seem to be working. — Кажется, кондиционер не работает.

luggage — неисчисляемое слово: не говорим luggages. Можно сказать my luggage или two bags.

READING · A2

The Ice Hotel ❄️

10–15 min

Read the text and complete the true/false and vocabulary tasks. The full reading is inside the dropdown so you can open it when you're ready.

Open the reading text + picture · The Ice Hotel

For many years the Arctic was a popular destination in the summer season to see the land of the midnight sun but in winter the few inhabitants had the snow and ice to themselves. By the end of the 1980s it was decided that the dark and cold winter should be seen as an advantage. In the winter of 1990 the French artist Jannot Derit was invited to have the opening of an exhibition in a specially built igloo (a building made of snow) in the little town of Jukkasjarvi on the frozen Torne River. The building, named Arctic Hall, attracted many interested visitors to the area. One night a group of foreign guests decided it would be a good idea to sleep in the Arctic Hall. The following morning the brave group were very pleased with their experience and the idea of an ice hotel was born. Today it is world famous.

As soon as winter begins, a team of snow builders, architects and artists from all over the world come to Jukkasjarvi and they make the hotel for that year. As one part is completed, it opens to visitors and overnight guests, while the other parts are still being built. The first part is completed in December and each week after that a new part opens, until January 7th when the hotel is completed. As the ICEHOTEL is built under the open sky, using the natural materials of the winter season, the finishing date depends on nature and therefore there are sometimes changes to the plan. In the spring, as the weather gets warmer, the hotel melts.

Inside the hotel, the temperature is never colder than -5°C to -8°C, however cold it may be outside. Winter outer clothes such as warm overalls, hats and gloves are included in the cost of guests' stay at the hotel. In addition to this, it is a good idea for guests to bring sweaters and a scarf as well as plenty of woollen socks and to choose footwear that is larger than normal to allow space for thick socks.

If you are planning to come to the hotel, you can buy warm sweaters, woollen socks and much more on the ICEHOTEL website. You can order these and the equipment you will need at the same time as you book your visit. The items will be delivered to your room when you check in.

The hotel is in the village of Jukkasjarvi, 200 km above the Arctic Circle but only 15 km from Kiruna airport and 17 km from Kiruna train station. Transport by bus can be arranged from the airport or train station to the ICEHOTEL.

Task A · True or False?

Choose YES or NO for each statement. The correct answers are shown in the answer key below.

1. During the 1980s, few tourists used to go to the Arctic in summer.
2. People came in large numbers to Jukkasjarvi to see the Arctic Hall.
3. The artist encouraged people to sleep in the Arctic Hall.
4. Each winter, guests come and sleep in the hotel before it is finished.
5. Progress when building the hotel is influenced by the weather.
6. The temperature inside the hotel changes according to the temperature outside.
7. Some clothes are provided by the hotel.
8. Guests should buy boots which fit as tightly as possible.
9. Items ordered through the ICEHOTEL shop will be delivered to your home.
10. It is possible to take a train from the airport to the ICEHOTEL.
Answer key + explanations
  1. NO. The Arctic was described as a popular summer destination.
  2. YES. Arctic Hall attracted many interested visitors.
  3. NO. The guests themselves decided to spend the night there.
  4. YES. Completed sections open while other parts are still being built.
  5. YES. The finishing date depends on nature and can change.
  6. NO. The text says the inside temperature stays within the stated range, however cold it is outside.
  7. YES. Warm overalls, hats and gloves are included in the stay.
  8. NO. Footwear should be larger than normal to make room for thick socks.
  9. NO. Items are delivered to guests' rooms when they check in.
  10. NO. Bus transport can be arranged from the airport or train station.

Task B · Choose the more natural phrase

Choose the better option for a polite hotel conversation.

  1. I've got a reservation (under my name / on my name).
  2. (Is breakfast included? / Is breakfast including?) in the price?
  3. Could I get two (bags / luggages), please?
  4. I'd like to (check out / check out my room) now.
  5. (Give me another room / Would it be possible to move to another room?)?
  6. Could I get a sandwich, (as well / also too)?
  7. The AC (doesn't seem to be working / doesn't seem working).
Answer key + why

under my name · Is breakfast included? · bags · check out · Would it be possible to move to another room? · as well · doesn't seem to be working. These phrases are natural and polite; luggage is uncountable, and “check out my room” means inspect/look at the room rather than leave the hotel.

⭐ Bonus grammar · keep it polite

Could I…? = вежливо попросить что-то: Could I leave my luggage here? — Можно оставить здесь багаж?

Would it be possible to…? = мягко спросить, возможно ли что-то: Would it be possible to move to another room? — Можно ли переселиться в другой номер?

Could you arrange + noun…? = попросить организовать что-то: Could you arrange a taxi for me? — Не могли бы вы заказать мне такси?

05 · role-play & video questions

Check in, solve it, check out 🗣️

15 min

Role-play · You're the guest

Scenario 1 — Check-in: You booked a twin room for two nights. Give your name, show your passport, and ask whether breakfast is included and what time it is served.

Scenario 2 — A problem: You arrive in your room. The AC doesn't work and there are no towels. Explain the problem politely and ask for help. If they can't fix the AC, ask whether you can move rooms.

Scenario 3 — Check-out: You want to check out, ask for the bill, pay by card, leave your luggage until 5 p.m., and arrange a taxi to the airport.

Challenge: Try to keep each conversation going for 1–2 minutes without reading full sentences.

Questions from the video 🎥

1. You arrive at a hotel. How would you tell the receptionist you have a reservation?
2. You enter your room, but the AC isn't working. What would you say to the receptionist?
3. You have a flight tomorrow morning at 6:30 a.m. How would you ask the hotel to arrange transportation to the airport?
