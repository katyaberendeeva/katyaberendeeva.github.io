02 · personal story

Alisa’s concert story 💫

12 min

Tell me about the Egor Kreed concert. Pick the questions that help you tell the story in your own way.

What was the concert like? Where was it?
How was the atmosphere? Was the crowd excited?
What did you like most? What do you remember best?
Which songs did he perform? Did you sing along?
What did you notice about the stage, lights or sound?
How did you spend time with your fiancé before or after the concert?
Was anything unexpected or especially funny?
Would you go to another concert by him? Why?
Tell it like a story: First… / Then… / My favourite part was… / After the concert… / Overall, it was…
03 · reading & vocabulary

A huge show in Jakarta 🌍

12 min

Read the short adapted text. Underline the details that help you imagine the concert.

Ye brings a giant show to Jakarta

On October 24, 2026, rapper Ye, formerly known as Kanye West, performed at a stadium in Jakarta. Around 50,000 fans came to the show. A huge globe was part of the stage, and bright lights and colourful images filled the stadium.

Ye performed some of his best-known songs, including “Stronger” and “Flashing Lights.” The crowd watched the show and sang along to familiar hits. For many fans, the giant stage and the visuals made the concert feel like a big spectacle.

People can enjoy a concert in different ways. Some focus on the music; others remember the lights, the crowd or the people they went with. What makes a concert unforgettable for you?

04 · practise the phrases

Make it sound natural 📝

8 min
Complete each sentence aloud
  1. The atmosphere was ______ because ______.
  2. My favourite part of the concert was ______.
  3. The crowd started to ______ when ______.
  4. The stage looked ______, and the lights were ______.
  5. I was impressed by ______.
  6. The concert was worth it because ______.
  7. I’d love to see ______ perform live because ______.
Choose the natural phrase

1. The singer performed / made three songs.   2. We sang along / sang together with our favourite song.   3. The show was sold out / sold away.

Check

performed three songs · sang along · sold out

05 · speaking cards

Tell it, feel it, imagine it 🎙️

10 min

Choose two cards. Speak for about a minute and try to use at least three new phrases.

📖 The storyTell the story of the Egor Kreed concert from the moment you arrived to the moment you went home.
💓 The emotionsDescribe how you felt before, during and after the concert. What changed your mood?
🔍 The detailsDescribe one detail you noticed: the crowd, the sound, the lights, the stage or the setlist.
✈️ The dream ticketIf you could fly anywhere for one concert, whose concert would you choose? Who would you take with you and why?
Speaking frame

I went to see… with… / The atmosphere was… / The crowd… / My favourite part was… / I was impressed by… / It was worth it because… / If I could fly anywhere, I’d go to…
